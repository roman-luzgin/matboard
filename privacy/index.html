Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

In short: Matboard edits your photos on your device. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

Matboard (“the app”) is an iOS app published by Roman Luzgin (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your photos

Matboard asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos, let you edit them, and save the results back to your library. All editing — cropping, framing, slicing, blending and batch processing — happens entirely on your device. Your photos and their metadata (such as location and camera details) are never uploaded, transmitted or shared with us or anyone else. When you save an edit, the new photo is written to your own Photos library; the original is not changed.

You can change or revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Matboard.

Data we collect

None. Matboard does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers or location data. It contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings and presets (export format, quality, saved frame presets) are stored locally on your device.
  • A count of free saves used is stored in your device’s keychain so the free allowance works correctly. It contains no personal information and never leaves your device.
  • A photo details index lets you filter your library by camera, lens and exposure settings. Once you open the filter, Matboard reads these details from your photos and keeps a copy on your device. It holds each photo’s camera, lens, ISO, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, file format, when it was last edited and its Photos identifier, but no images or locations.
  • Your recent posts: the photos you pick in Preview to compare new posts against are remembered by their Photos identifiers, so they’re there next time. Removing them in Preview forgets them.

Deleting the app removes your settings, presets, the photo details index and your recent posts. The free-save count may remain in your device keychain.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (“Matboard Pro”) is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Accounts

Matboard has no user accounts, so there is no account to create, link or delete.

Children

Matboard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
