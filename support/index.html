Support

Something not working, or an idea for Matboard? We’d love to hear it.

Get in touch

Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Matboard’s Settings).

Common questions

How many free saves do I get?

20 saved photos. Each photo saved to your library counts once — a 3-panel carousel uses 3. The remaining count is shown in the library and in Settings.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Matboard change my original photos?

No. Every save creates a new photo (optionally in a “Matboard” album) and leaves the original untouched.

Matboard can’t see my photos.

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Matboard and choose Full Access, or tap Add more in the library if you chose limited access.

Do you upload my photos or collect data?

No. Everything happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Matboard has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign up for or delete.
